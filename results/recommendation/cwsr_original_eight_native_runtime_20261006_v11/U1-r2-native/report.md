### 候选资料：che-2021-0.8m-pc-emc（通过筛选）
以下为候选记录中的组成，配制时须核对所引原文的比例基准与用量。
盐：NaPF6；浓度：0.8 mol L⁻¹；NaPF6：0.8 mol L⁻¹；溶剂：PC, EMC；溶剂比例（体积）：PC=1.0, EMC=1.0；添加剂：FEC；添加剂用量：FEC=2 wt%。
- 配方原文 [0005_Che 等 - 2021 - Engineering optimization approach of nonaqueous electrolyte for sodium ion battery with long cycle l::sec8::child1]：Therefore, in the present work, the PC/EMC solvent system was preferably selected to investigate the effect of solvent ratio and salt concentration. In the binary PC/EMC (1:1, v/v) system, the highest conductivity of 7.45 mS cm $^{-1}$ at 20 °C is observed at 0.8 M NaPF $_{6}$ (Fig. 1b). It is also found that the conductivity of electrolyte changes with the solvent ratio changing. The viscosity of 0.8 M NaPF $_{6}$ electrolyte is gradually increased with the PC content rising. With an increase in the PC content, the conductivity increases markedly in the range lower than 40%, reaches a maximum at 60% (i.e., PC/EMC=3/2), and inversely declines in the range higher than 60% (Fig. 1c). On the other hand, the electrolytes with PC content lower than 40% are much easier to wet the nonpolar polypropylene-polyethylene separator. Complete wetting of the separator typically takes no more than 10 seconds, as indicated by the wetting angles and wettability in Fig. S1 and Fig. S2. Based on the above results, five 0.8 M NaPF $_{6}$ electrolytes were prepared by using 2 wt% FEC additive and PC/EMC, PC/DMC, EC/EMC, EC/DMC and EC/DEC mixed solvents (all having a 2:3 cyclic/linear carbonate ratio), respectively. It is shown that the ionic conductivity of these electrolytes is increased in the order of EC/DMC > PC/DMC > EC/EMC > PC/EMC > EC/DEC, whereas the viscosity is changed nearly in an opposite order of EC/DEC > EC/EMC > PC/EMC > PC/DMC ≈ EC/DMC (Fig. 2a).

NaDFP was added to the baseline electrolyte to prepare. All electrolytes were prepared in a dry argon filled glove box with both moisture and oxygen concentrations below 0.1 ppm. NFM was purchased from Jiangsu Xiangying New Energy Technology Co, Ltd and Hard Carbon were purchased from Kuraray Co Ltd. The cathode-separator-anode assembles were first prepared, and then stacked with 10 cathode electrodes by 11 anode electrodes and packaged in a plastic-laminated aluminum pouch. The resultant pouch cell was dried under vacuum at 80 °C for 72 h before being moved into a glove box for injecting electrolyte. The size of prepared pouch cells was 52 mm × 5 mm × 100 mm.
