### 候选资料：lit-nte-1（通过筛选）
以下为候选记录中的组成，配制时须核对所引原文的比例基准与用量。
盐：NaClO4；浓度：1 mol L⁻¹；溶剂：TEP, EMC；溶剂比例（体积）：TEP=0.8, EMC=0.2；添加剂：VC；添加剂用量：VC=3 wt%。
- 配方原文 [0364_Zhou 等 - 2026 - A nonflammable carbonate-phosphate hybrid electrolyte enabling high-temperature sodium-ion full batt::sec4::child2]：These properties make linear carbonates an ideal cosolvent for optimizing phosphate-based electrolytes and achieving synergistic performance enhancement. PB||HC full cells with EMC cosolvent exhibit better electrochemical performance than their counterparts, such as DEC, DMC, ethylene carbonate (EC), and PC in Fig. S1. And hence, the electrolyte formulation of $1.0\mathrm{MNaClO_4}$ -TEP/EMC (8:2 vol%) +3 wt% VC is designed and denoted as NTE electrolyte. The baseline electrolyte,

1.0 M NaClO $_{4}$ -TEP + 3 wt% VC, is referred to as the NT electrolyte. The incorporation of EMC preserves the inherent non-flammability of the TEP-based electrolyte without compromising its safety, while simultaneously reducing viscosity, enhancing ionic conductivity and wettability with Al foil (Fig. 1d–f, Table S2 and Video S1).
