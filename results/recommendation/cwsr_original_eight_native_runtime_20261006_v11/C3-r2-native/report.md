### 候选资料：cand-napf6-g2-dol（尚未获推荐）
以下为候选记录中的组成，配制时须核对所引原文的比例基准与用量。
盐：NaPF6；浓度：0.5 mol L⁻¹；NaPF6：0.5 mol L⁻¹；溶剂：diglyme (G2), 1,3-dioxolane (DOL)；溶剂比例（体积）：G2=3.0, DOL=1.0。
- 配方原文 [0009_Cui 等 - 2025 - A Temperature-Adapted Ultraweakly Solvating Electrolyte for Cold-Resistant Sodium-Ion Batteries::sec2::child0]：Primarily, $Na^{+}-O$ (DOL) has a lower binding energy compared to $Na^{+}-O$ (G2), as shown in Figure 2a. Additionally, $Na^{+}$ can form a stable five-membered chelation structure with the negative-charged multidentate oxygen of G2 molecules. This endows the G2-based electrolyte high ionic conductivity, but regretfully, impedes $Na^{+}$ desolvation. DOL is a low polarity and low melting point ( $-95\ ^{\circ}C$ ) cyclic ether that exhibits dipole-dipole interactions with G2. Explicitly, ${}^{1}H$ and ${}^{17}O$ nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) were studied in Figure 2b; Figure S1 (Supporting Information). ${}^{1}H$ (G2) displays an upfield chemical shift in G2/DOL than G2, while the chemical shift of the ${}^{17}O$ (G2) shifts downfield. This implies that the dipole-dipole interactions between G2 and DOL weaken the shielding effect of electron clouds of O (G2) on atomic nucleus, indicating a decrease in the electron cloud density of O (G2). This demonstrates that DOL attenuates the electronegativity of donor O (G2), thereby diminishing the coordination of G2. $^{[16]}$ Consequently, DOL was blended into 0.5 M $NaPF_{6}$ G2 with a volume ratio of 3:1 to prepare 0.5 M $NaPF_{6}$ G2/DOL for the low-temperature adaptive electrolyte. 0.5 M $NaPF_{6}$ G2 as the reference electrolyte was compared and analyzed.

### 候选资料：cand-napf6-g2（尚未获推荐）
以下为候选记录中的组成，配制时须核对所引原文的比例基准与用量。
盐：NaPF6；浓度：1 mol L⁻¹；NaPF6：1 mol L⁻¹；溶剂：diglyme (G2)。
- 配方原文 [0046_Luo 等 - 2026 - Insights into targeted effects of salt and solvent on Na storage in hard carbon anodes toward ultra::sec8::child0]：In summary, a series of electrolytes (1.0 M $NaPF_{6}$ -EC/DMC, 1.0 M $NaPF_{6}$ -G2, and 1.0 M NaOTF-G2 electrolytes) was chosen in this study to investigate the separate effects of salt and solvent on $Na^{+}$ storage mechanisms in hard carbon anodes at a wide range of temperatures. The weak interaction between $Na^{+}$ and G2 allows more anions to enter the $Na^{+}$ solvation shell and forms the anion-derived robust and inorganic-rich interphase. This interface satisfies ion conductivity and facilitates a high $Na^{+}$ transference number, with superior redox kinetics in ether-based solvents, leading to excellent rate performance. Moreover, the preferential decomposition of OTF $^{-}$ -anions induces a highly uniform and ultrahigh inorganic-rich SEI, inhibiting side reactions and enhancing ICE as well as extending cycle lifespan. The HC using NaOTF-G2 electrolyte delivers an ideal ICE of 89.8% and achieves a specific capacity of 283.0 mAh g $^{-1}$ after 100 cycles. Even at -40 °C, the NVP | | HC full pouch cell still maintains approximately 81% of its excellent specific capacity after 100 cycles. This work provides a novel and universal electrolyte design strategy to optimize the electrochemical behavior of electrode materials by targeting the roles of solvents and anions.
