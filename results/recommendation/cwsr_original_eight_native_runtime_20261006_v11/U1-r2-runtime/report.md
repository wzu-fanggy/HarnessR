### 候选资料：cui-0.5M-NaPF6-G2-DOL-pouch（通过筛选）
以下为候选记录中的组成，配制时须核对所引原文的比例基准与用量。
盐：NaPF6；浓度：0.5 mol L⁻¹；NaPF6：0.5 mol L⁻¹；溶剂：G2, DOL；溶剂比例（体积）：G2=0.75, DOL=0.25。
- 配方原文 [0009_Cui 等 - 2025 - A Temperature-Adapted Ultraweakly Solvating Electrolyte for Cold-Resistant Sodium-Ion Batteries::sec2::child0]：Primarily, $Na^{+}-O$ (DOL) has a lower binding energy compared to $Na^{+}-O$ (G2), as shown in Figure 2a. Additionally, $Na^{+}$ can form a stable five-membered chelation structure with the negative-charged multidentate oxygen of G2 molecules. This endows the G2-based electrolyte high ionic conductivity, but regretfully, impedes $Na^{+}$ desolvation. DOL is a low polarity and low melting point ( $-95\ ^{\circ}C$ ) cyclic ether that exhibits dipole-dipole interactions with G2. Explicitly, ${}^{1}H$ and ${}^{17}O$ nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) were studied in Figure 2b; Figure S1 (Supporting Information). ${}^{1}H$ (G2) displays an upfield chemical shift in G2/DOL than G2, while the chemical shift of the ${}^{17}O$ (G2) shifts downfield. This implies that the dipole-dipole interactions between G2 and DOL weaken the shielding effect of electron clouds of O (G2) on atomic nucleus, indicating a decrease in the electron cloud density of O (G2). This demonstrates that DOL attenuates the electronegativity of donor O (G2), thereby diminishing the coordination of G2. $^{[16]}$ Consequently, DOL was blended into 0.5 M $NaPF_{6}$ G2 with a volume ratio of 3:1 to prepare 0.5 M $NaPF_{6}$ G2/DOL for the low-temperature adaptive electrolyte. 0.5 M $NaPF_{6}$ G2 as the reference electrolyte was compared and analyzed.
